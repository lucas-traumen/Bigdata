Policy check:
  replica 1 on the writing client node        : PASS
  replica 2 on a DIFFERENT rack than the client: PASS (n5 in r2)
  replica 3 on ANOTHER node of rack r2         : PASS (n4 in r2)

Q1: what if the cluster has only ONE rack?
  IndexError: Cannot choose from an empty sequence
  -> raised at random.choice(other_racks): that list is EMPTY
     because no node lives outside rack r1.
  Why this is dangerous for fault tolerance: every replica would
  sit on the same rack; one rack-level failure (top-of-rack switch,
  power circuit) destroys ALL copies at once - replication by rack
  is the whole point of the policy.

Q2: RF = 5 with at most 2 replicas per rack
  Strategy: 1 replica on the client node, then 2 nodes from EACH
  of two other racks -> 5 replicas over 3 racks, max 2 per rack.
  replica on n1 (rack r1)
  replica on n4 (rack r2)
  replica on n6 (rack r2)
  replica on n8 (rack r3)
  replica on n7 (rack r3)
  replicas per rack: {'r1': 1, 'r2': 2, 'r3': 2} (rule 'at most 2 per rack': PASS)
  Note: with only 2 racks this is impossible without breaking the
  rule - 5 replicas over 2 racks force 3 replicas on some rack.
