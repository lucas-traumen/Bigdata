[Bai ve nha] notes on the reliability trade-off:
- RF=2 tolerates only ONE DataNode failure; two concurrent failures
  can lose data. RF=3 tolerates two concurrent failures.
- EC(6+3) still tolerates 3 concurrent block losses, so in the
  hybrid plan the cold 80% is SAFER than the hot 20% (RF=2).
- Saving drops from 40% (main exercise) to 20%: with RF=2 the
  baseline already costs less, so EC adds less relative saving.
- RF=2 risks availability too: while one replica is down, only one
  copy serves reads and another failure window opens.
